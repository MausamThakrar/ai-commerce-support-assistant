
# Returns and Refunds Policy

Store: Northstar Goods

## Return Window
Eligible products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## Return Conditions
Products must be unused, clean, and returned with original packaging and accessories.

## Non-Returnable Items
Personalized products and gift cards are not eligible for standard returns.

## Return Shipping
Customers pay return shipping unless the product is defective or the wrong item was delivered.

## Refund Processing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection.

## Damaged or Incorrect Items
Customers must contact support within 7 days of delivery with their order number and photos.

## Exchanges
Direct exchanges are not supported.
Customers can return eligible products and place a new order.
