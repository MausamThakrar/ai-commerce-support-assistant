
# Order Cancellation Policy

Store: Northstar Goods

## Before Fulfillment
Orders can be cancelled before fulfillment begins.

## After Fulfillment Begins
Once fulfillment has started, cancellation is not guaranteed.
Customers must contact support.

## Shipped Orders
Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled.
Customers can request a return after delivery if the items are eligible.

## Refunds for Cancelled Orders
Approved cancellations are refunded to the original payment method.
Refund processing can take 5–10 business days.

## Order Modifications
Shipping addresses and order items cannot be changed through self-service after checkout.
Customers must contact support before fulfillment begins.
