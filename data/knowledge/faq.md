
# Frequently Asked Questions

Store: Northstar Goods

## Do I need an account to order?
No. Guest checkout is supported.

## How can I track my order?
Use the tracking link sent by email after dispatch.

## Can I change my delivery address?
Contact support before fulfillment begins.
Address changes are not guaranteed once processing has started.

## What payment methods are accepted?
Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

## Can I combine discount codes?
Only one discount code can be used per order.

## Do products have warranties?
Some products include a manufacturing warranty.
Check the individual product information for warranty duration.

## Can I cancel an order?
Cancellation is available before fulfillment begins.
Once an order has shipped, the return policy applies.

## How do I contact support?
Email support@northstar.example.
This address is fictional and used for demonstration only.
