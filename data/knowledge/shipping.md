
# Shipping Policy

Store: Northstar Goods

## Processing Times
Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days.
Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

## Standard Shipping
Standard shipping costs €4.90.
Estimated delivery is 3–5 business days after dispatch.

## Express Shipping
Express shipping costs €9.90.
Estimated delivery is 1–2 business days after dispatch.

## Free Shipping
Orders with a merchandise subtotal of €75 or more qualify for free standard shipping.

## Order Tracking
Customers receive a tracking link by email once their order is dispatched.
Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become available.

## International Shipping
Shipping is available within Germany and Austria.
Other destinations are not supported in this demo store.

## Delayed Orders
If an order has not arrived within 7 business days after dispatch,
customers can contact support for assistance.
